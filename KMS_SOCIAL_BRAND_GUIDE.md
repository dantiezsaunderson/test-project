KMS Social Brand Guide (Working Draft)
======================================
Style: Detroit grit + editorial modernism. Clean, bold, timeless.

Logo System
-----------
Primary (Wordmark):
- assets/logos/kms-wordmark-modern.svg
- Use on dark backgrounds with accent gold rule.

Secondary (Stacked):
- assets/logos/kms-wordmark-stacked.svg
- Use for square formats and cover cards.

Monogram:
- assets/logos/kms-monogram-block.svg
- assets/logos/kms-monogram-outline.svg
- Use for avatars, reel covers, and corner stamps.

Clear Space
-----------
- Minimum clear space: 8% of logo width on all sides.
- Avoid placing over busy imagery without a dark overlay.

Color Palette
-------------
- Black: #0B0C10
- Charcoal: #151824
- Grit Gray: #2A2F3A
- Accent Gold: #F2C14E
- Offwhite: #F4F6FB
- Muted: #A5ACB8

Typography
----------
- Headline: Space Grotesk (700/800)
- Body: Inter (400/500)
- Caption / Meta: IBM Plex Mono (400/500)

Texture + Imagery
-----------------
- Use grain/noise at 8-12% for archival unification.
- High contrast on black, avoid neon.
- Use monochrome overlays for historic photos.

Layout Rules
------------
- Safe margin: 80px inset on 1080px canvases.
- Maintain consistent logo position (top-left or bottom-right).
- Keep copy under 12 words for main cards.
