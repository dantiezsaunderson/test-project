KMS Hashtag + SEO Strategy
==========================
Goal: Make the Detroit story discoverable without sounding trendy.

Caption SEO
-----------
- Put "KMS Records" and "Detroit" in the first 120 characters.
- Use clear keywords: "Detroit techno", "techno history", "underground".
- Add release titles and years once verified.
- Always credit photographers/designers in captions.

Hashtag Strategy (Rotate 6–10)
------------------------------
Core:
- #KMSRecords
- #DetroitTechno
- #DetroitMusic
- #TechnoHistory

Community:
- #UndergroundTechno
- #VinylCulture
- #TechnoCulture
- #DetroitLegacy

Content-Type:
- #RecordLabel
- #ArchiveSeries
- #TechnoReels
- #VinylCommunity

Do not:
- Stack 20+ hashtags
- Use unrelated or trending tags
- Repeat the exact same set daily

Alt Text Guidance
-----------------
Write one sentence describing the visual and the historical context:
Example: "Archival KMS Records flyer from Detroit with bold typography and date stamp."
