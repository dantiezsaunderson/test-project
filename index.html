Series Concept: KMS Legacy Capsules

A weekly capsule that highlights a key year, release, artist, or cultural moment. Use archival visuals, a short narrative, and a consistent template to build recall.

  • One hero image or flyer
  • Three supporting frames (detail, quote, tracklist)
  • Short audio clip or reel teaser

3) Acquisition & Rights Workflow

  1. Locate archives — KMS vault, personal archives, photographers, press kits.
  2. Digitize — Scan flyers/photos at 600dpi, save as TIFF + web JPG.
  3. Clear usage — Confirm credits and permission for every asset.
  4. Document — Track source, owner, and usage terms in a spreadsheet.

Keep proof of permission and always attribute photographers and designers in captions.

4) Production Workflow

Template System

  • Create 3-4 base templates (capsule, quote, timeline, catalog)
  • Lock a consistent grid, fonts, and logo lockup
  • Use one accent color per quarter to keep it fresh

Quality Control

  • Check crop safety on all sizes
  • Use grain or texture to unify mixed archives
  • Ensure copy is short and readable on mobile

5) Post Specs (HQ + Consistency)

Feed

  • 1080x1350 (4:5) primary
  • 1080x1080 (1:1) secondary
  • PNG for graphics, JPG for photos

Reels

  • 1080x1920 (9:16)
  • 30fps, H.264, 10-15s minimum
  • Captions burned-in for silence

Stories

  • 1080x1920 with safe margins
  • Headline + 1 CTA link/sticker
  • Use archival b-roll and quick cuts

6) Publishing Cadence (Starter Plan)

Weekly Rhythm

  • Mon: Legacy Capsule (feed carousel)
  • Wed: Artist clip or quote (reel)
  • Fri: Catalog spotlight or teaser

Monthly Beats

  • One long-form recap or mini-doc
  • One collaboration or remix highlight
  • Archive deep dive for superfans
