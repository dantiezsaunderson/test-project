Grid martingale strategy overview

A structured approach built for XAUUSD and major FX pairs with clear entry logic and protection.

Key features

  • Multiple entry strategies (Candle Direction recommended).
  • Dual basket support (long and short simultaneously).
  • Per-basket drawdown tracking.
  • News filtering and volatility protection.
  • Smart risk management controls.

Entry strategy (5m candle)

  • Wait for the 5m candle to close.
  • Follow the previous candle direction for the first entry.
  • Build the grid only when price moves against the basket.
  • Close baskets when target profit is reached.

Risk controls

  • Per-basket drawdown caps and exposure limits.
  • News filter to pause during high-impact events.
  • Volatility filter to avoid extreme spikes.
  • Clear stop and recovery rules for baskets.

Best markets

  • Gold (XAUUSD).
  • Major FX pairs (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY).
  • Avoid illiquid hours and major news spikes.
  • Works best with consistent spreads.
Risk note: Grid and martingale strategies increase exposure during adverse moves. Copy responsibly and use appropriate account sizing.

Daily micro updates and a weekly recap are enough. Consistency matters more than volume.

Avoid promises or guarantees. Focus on process, risk, and verified outcomes.

Is grid/martingale risky?

Yes. It can increase exposure during adverse moves. Use proper sizing, drawdown limits, and avoid high-impact news.

How do I build trust quickly?

Share your rules, drawdown limits, and trade rationale. Reply to questions fast.

What is a good first offer?
