How Clawd Bot works

  1. Ingest: connect prices, order books, on-chain data, and news feeds.
  2. Analyze: detect regime shifts, anomalies, and sentiment velocity.
  3. Assist: summarize context, build watchlists, and draft alerts.
  4. Act: route to paper or live execution with guardrails.

Use Clawd bot as a research copilot. You decide the trades, it handles the signal flow.
