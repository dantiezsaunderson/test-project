KMS Records Key Quotes (From User Dossier)
===========================================
Format:
QUOTE | SPEAKER | SOURCE | YEAR | NOTES

"Juan had Metroplex, Derrick had Transmat... I could do that. I wanted to do it myself... I wanted to control what I did with my record... I didn't want nobody telling me how to put my music out... I wanted to control that." | Kevin Saunderson | RBMA lecture | 2018 | KMS origin story

"This track was my first release on KMS - it was the beginning. The two gentlemen who inspired me to make music and start my own label are Juan Atkins and Derrick May... Juan would come round and help me figure out how to mix them down... Following that I had my first release on KMS." | Kevin Saunderson | Fabric London "Crate Diggin'" | 2018 | On "Triangle of Love"

"Chez Damier was my A&R in KMS's early years... Another protege of mine was MK... 'Can You Feel It' (MK's Dub) ... what a slamming record... Now it's probably one of our biggest classics on KMS." | Kevin Saunderson | Fabric London "Crate Diggin'" | 2018 | A&R and MK context

"Another classic... Detroit was truly vibing with young talent... we all shared a block of studios and record companies: Metroplex, Transmat and KMS. It was like a big family." | Kevin Saunderson | Fabric London (R-Tyme / Carl Craig remix note) | 2018 (verify) | Detroit studio block

"Idyllic... one of the first collabs I had done in years... the perfect track to lead our 30th Anniversary EPs for KMS - a combination of disco, house and techno." | Kevin Saunderson | Fabric London | 2017 | 30th Anniversary EPs

"Operating in tandem to his golden run as a producer in the late '80s and early '90s, Saunderson's own KMS record label - 30 years strong in 2017 - has chronicled the early footsteps of R-Tyme, Blake Baxter, MK, Chez Damier, Derrick Carter, Bicep and even Saunderson's son Dantiez." | Armada Music editorial | 2017 | External perspective
