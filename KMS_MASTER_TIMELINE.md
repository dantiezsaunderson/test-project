## KMS Records Master Timeline

Status: Updated from the user-provided dossier (2026-02-03). Cross-check with
primary sources before publishing or printing.

### Origin + Mission (User-Provided)
- Founded: 1987 in Detroit by Kevin Saunderson.
- Name: KMS = Kevin Maurice Saunderson.
- Origin story: Saunderson built a studio in his apartment after college and
  launched KMS to keep creative control and avoid outside timelines, influenced
  by Metroplex and Transmat.
- Mission: Primary outlet for Saunderson's projects and aliases (Inner City,
  Reese, Kreem, E-Dancer, Tronik House, Reese Project, etc.) and a platform to
  develop Detroit talent, blending machine-driven techno with soulful vocals
  and house grooves.

### Cultural Context (User-Provided)
- The Belleville Three (Atkins, May, Saunderson) built parallel labels:
  Metroplex, Transmat, KMS.
- Early labels and studios were on the same block, creating a shared hub often
  described as a Techno Boulevard artists' colony.
- KMS blended Detroit techno grit with soulful, vocal-forward house crossover.

### Visual Evolution (User-Provided)
- Early 12-inch labels show the KMS mark and Ypsilanti address.
- DIY era: limited runs, local pressing (Archer), and hand-delivered test
  pressings to Detroit clubs.
- Flyers and posters carried gritty, futurist typography and high-contrast
  layouts.
